I'm building a custom recommendation engine because platforms like YouTube Music often fail to capture why I like a specific part of a song. My goal is to recommend songs based on their actual melodic structure rather than what other users are listening to. The system works by tracking the relative changes in musical notes (the intervals). It's tempo-agnostic, meaning it looks for the same sequence of net pitch changes even if the notes are held for different lengths of time. Ultimately, it matches the overall direction and shape of the melody to find songs with the exact same musical vibe.
